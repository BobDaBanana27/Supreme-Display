# Mechanical Enclosure Design & CAD Specifications

## Dimensions & Tolerances
- **Overall Envelope:** 242.0 mm (W) x 182.0 mm (H) x 8.8 mm (D).
- **Active Window:** 210.0 mm x 157.0 mm (matching 10.3" 4:3 panel).
- **Shell Wall Thickness:** 1.6 mm (4 perimeters with 0.4mm nozzle).
- **Assembly Method:** 6x M2.5 brass heat-set threaded inserts with countersunk machine screws + perimeter snap-fit tabs.

## Layer Stackup (Front to Back)
1. **Front Bezel Shell:** Textured PLA/PETG with 0.8mm display lip.
2. **E-Paper Display Module:** 10.3" glass substrate with matte surface finish.
3. **Internal Mounting Chassis:** Mid-plate providing rigidity and component separation.
4. **EMR Sensor Grid:** Adhesive-bonded digitizer FPC positioned directly behind the display.
5. **Main PCB & Sub-Modules:** ESP32-S3 board, SD adapter, and TP4056 mounted on 2.0mm standoffs.
6. **LiPo Battery:** 3500 mAh pouch cell secured with high-bond adhesive tape.
7. **Rear Cover Shell:** Integrated kickstand mount and side stylus silo with magnetic retention.
